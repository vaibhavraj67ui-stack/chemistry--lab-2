Formula Bank

विलयन (Solutions)

M = n / V(L)

M = molarity, n = solute ke moles, V = solution ka volume in litre.

m = n / mass of solvent (kg)

Molality mein solvent ka mass kg mein hota hai.

p = x × p°

Raoult's law: volatile component ka partial vapour pressure.

विद्युत रसायन (Electrochemistry)

E°cell = E°cathode − E°anode

ΔG° = −nFE°cell

log K = nE°cell / 0.0591 (at 298 K)

Last formula 298 K par Nernst relation se.

रासायनिक बलगतिकी (Chemical Kinetics)

Rate = −Δ[R] / Δt

k = (2.303/t) log(a/(a−x))

First-order reaction ke liye half-life: t½ = 0.693/k

Colligative properties

ΔTb = Kb × m

ΔTf = Kf × m

π = CRT

Dilute solution ke liye osmotic pressure formula.

Quick unit reminder

Temperature difference: K; volume in molarity: L; molality: mol kg⁻¹; Faraday constant F ≈ 96500 C mol⁻¹.

ORGANIC QUICK REVISION

Important Reactions

1. Williamson ether synthesis

R–ONa + R′–X → R–O–R′ + NaX

Alkoxide aur primary alkyl halide se ether banaya ja sakta hai.

2. Aldol condensation

α-hydrogen wale aldehyde/ketone dilute base mein β-hydroxy carbonyl compound bana sakte hain; heating par dehydration hoti hai.

3. Cannizzaro reaction

α-hydrogen na rakhne wale aldehydes concentrated alkali mein oxidation-reduction dete hain.

4. Haloform reaction

CH₃CO– group wale methyl ketones, I₂/NaOH ke saath yellow CHI₃ precipitate de sakte hain.

5. Esterification

Carboxylic acid + alcohol ⇌ ester + water (conc. H₂SO₄, heat).

6. Reimer–Tiemann reaction

Phenol + CHCl₃/NaOH, followed by acid work-up → mainly o-hydroxybenzaldehyde.

7. Sandmeyer reaction

Aryl diazonium salt se CuCl/CuBr/CuCN ki help se aryl halide/cyanide ban sakta hai.

8. Hoffmann bromamide reaction

Amide + Br₂/NaOH → primary amine with one carbon less.

Exam note: reaction ke saath reagent/condition aur product bhi yaad karo.
